Five focused stages

Simple, collaborative, and built around good decisions.

The exact details can flex with the scope, but every project follows the same clear progression.

01

Consultation

We start with your business: what you offer, who you serve, what is working, and where your current online presence falls short.

Outcome: a shared understanding of the project
02

Design

The content hierarchy and visual direction take shape. You will see how the site communicates, moves, and guides a potential customer.

Outcome: an approved website direction
03

Development

The approved design becomes a responsive, accessible website. Interactions, screen sizes, forms, and finishing details are built and tested.

Outcome: a complete working website
04

Review

You review the complete experience in context. Feedback is collected, prioritized, and refined so the final result feels ready and right.

Outcome: final approval for launch
05

Launch

After the final checks, the website moves into its live home. We make sure the essential handoff details are clear before the project closes.

Outcome: a polished new online presence

Before we begin

Process questions.

What should I prepare before the consultation?

Come with a basic sense of your goals, the services you want to feature, and any current website or brand materials you have. It is fine if the details are still rough.

How involved do I need to be?

Your insight is most valuable at a few focused checkpoints: discovery, design direction, and final review. The process is designed to make those moments clear and productive.

What if I need help organizing my content?

That is common. Content structure is part of the work, so we can turn a loose collection of services and ideas into a clear page plan.

How long does a website project take?

Timing depends on scope, content readiness, and the speed of feedback. You will receive a project-specific schedule once the right approach is clear.
